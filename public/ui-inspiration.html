Recommended direction

Growing Garden colors + Bright Ledger structure

Shared design principles

Color follows meaning

Brand, save, spend, interest, and status each get a stable role in both themes.

Stable structure, playful content

Keep navigation and forms calm; put delight into goals, progress, and teaching moments.

Progress beats raw totals

Show movement toward a goal and explain what changed, not only the current balance.

Agency over casino mechanics

Use prediction and experimentation; avoid streak pressure, risky celebrations, and trading cues.

Two densities, one family

Parents and children need different information density, not unrelated visual brands. Preserve the same semantic colors and component language on both sides.
